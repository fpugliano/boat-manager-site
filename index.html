Clearance

Your legal status, at a glance.

Non-EU boats in Greece juggle Transit Log validity, eTEPAY payments, and the Schengen 90/180-day rolling window simultaneously. Miss one date and it costs thousands. The Clearance tab shows every countdown on one screen — green, amber, or red. No spreadsheet hunting the night before you check in.

Engine Maintenance

Never miss a service interval again.

Track port and starboard engine hours separately. Every service item — engine oil, gear oil, fuel filters, lip seals — gets its own gauge showing exactly how many hours remain before the next change. Time-based tasks like coolant flushes show a day countdown alongside the hour-based ones. You'll never pull into a boatyard having to say "I think it was due around…"

Provisions

One list. Everyone on board sees it.

Build your provisioning inventory by category — food, drinks, toiletries, and more. A shopping list shows exactly what needs restocking. Check off items as you shop; your crew sees updates in real time without a phone call. History and insights tabs show what you've stocked before, so reprovisioning for a long passage takes minutes, not an afternoon.

Spare Parts

Know exactly what's on board. Before you need it.

Catalog every spare by manufacturer part number, quantity, storage location, and cost paid. Filter by system — Yanmar engine, saildrive, watermaker, electrical. Adjust stock counts with a tap. When a part drops low it flags red, so you order before the next crossing — not after you've already left the last chandlery three weeks behind you.

Systems

Your entire boat, documented from keel to masthead.

Log every installed system — electrical, mechanical, sails, rigging — with full specs: model numbers, wiring notes, install dates, prices paid, and exact location on board. When a contractor asks what shunt you're running with the LiFePO4 bank, or a surveyor wants the sail inventory, you pull it up in seconds instead of digging through a folder of receipts.

LPG

How much gas is left. What you paid for it.

Track every bottle on board by size and log refills as you go. The dashboard shows total kilograms remaining, how many bottles are full, and a running price history — lowest, average, and highest cost per kilogram across every port you've ever filled in. Useful for comparing marina prices between countries, and for knowing if you'll make it to the next port without running dry mid-passage.

Water Maker

Don't guess when to change the filters.

Log your current hourmeter reading and the reading at your last filter change. The gauge calculates hours remaining to the next membrane service automatically — no mental arithmetic, no sticky note on the nav table. A separate charcoal filter tracker runs on a 6-month calendar interval, so you know when it's due even if the watermaker hasn't been used much since the last crossing.

Winterize

Every winterization task, in the right order.

A full seasonal checklist organised into sections — general tasks, engine, rig, systems — with 58 items for Winter 2025/26 and counting. Reset each spring and start fresh for the new season. Add custom tasks specific to your boat. Work through the list anchored in the boatyard or hand it off to your yard manager so nothing gets missed while you're not there.

Settings

Your tabs, your data, your rules.

The AI Import Assistant lives here — take a photo of any document or paste text and it maps the data to the right tab automatically. Choose exactly which tabs appear and drag them into the order that makes sense for how you sail. Cloud sync keeps every device current. Backup and restore any time, or import from JSON if you're migrating from a spreadsheet.

Your boat, your tabs.

Sailing the Pacific? Hide the Greek paperwork tabs. Focused on provisioning for a long passage? Put Provisions front and center. Show only what matters to you, in the order you want it.
