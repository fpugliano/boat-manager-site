The problem

Boats break. Notes get lost.

Managing a bluewater cruiser means juggling hundreds of systems, schedules, and repairs across months at sea — usually with no cell service.

📋

Scattered notebooks

Maintenance notes live in three different notebooks, a WhatsApp thread, and someone's memory. Good luck finding the impeller replacement date.

🗓️

Missed service intervals

You know the watermaker filter needs replacing every 200 hours, but you haven't been tracking hours. Was that last replacement in Grenada or Trinidad?

🏪

Lost receipts & manuals

The warranty card for the windlass is somewhere on the boat. The manual for the autopilot? Haven't seen it since Cape Town.

👥

Crew handoffs

Your delivery crew fixed something while you were flying home. Now you have no idea what was changed, what was sourced, or what still needs attention.

The solution

Everything in one place,
everywhere you sail.

Task & maintenance tracking

Create tasks for any system on the boat — engine, rigging, electronics, safety gear. Set intervals, log completions, and always know what's due and what's overdue.

  • Recurring task schedules (by date or engine hours)
  • Status tracking: pending, in progress, done
  • Notes and cost logging per completion

Photo documentation

Attach photos to any task or repair. Document the state of a fitting before and after, capture serial numbers, store receipts. Your visual boat log, always at hand.

  • Attach images to any task or note
  • Stored securely in the cloud
  • Access from any device

Encrypted cloud sync

Your data is encrypted before it ever leaves your device. Only you — with your PIN — can read it. We can't see your data, and neither can anyone else.

  • AES-GCM encryption, client-side
  • PIN-protected access
  • Syncs across all your devices
